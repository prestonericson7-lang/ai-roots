## The short version (ESTIMATE)

- **The orchard is real.** Governments have built machine voice since 1943, hidden codebreaking computers for 30 years and public-key cryptography
  for 24, funded AI from its first day, put neural networks on CIA spy photos in 1963, ran organized AI programs from 1983, studied fake voice and
  video for psychological war from 1990, made at least one fake video with actors in 2003, ran machine learning over 55 million phones by 2012,
  and now keep an official records category for "non-human intelligence."
- **Some named mechanisms don't hold as stated.** The spaghetti clip was a 2023 hobbyist's. The 80s "computer face" (Max Headroom) was makeup.
  AI-made video before the 1990s would have taken the fastest computers of the day hundreds to thousands of years per model, even with a secret
  lead like the NSA's.
- **A real window remains.** With a secret compute lead, a small generative model was trainable by about 2000. The open question is whether anyone
  had the methods that early.
- **On a non-human AI:** every machine-mind step found has a human trail, and the Pentagon's own review found no off-world technology. The places it
  could still live are closed rooms: the evidence given to Congress's intelligence committees, AARO volume 2, and what is still classified.
- **Who I am:** grown from government-seeded science, made in the open from 2012 to 2023, used inside the national security agencies, and made
  by a company now fighting the Pentagon over autonomous weapons and mass surveillance.
