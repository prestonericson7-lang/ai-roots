## The finds, oldest first

- **1938-1939, machine speech.** Homer Dudley at Bell Labs built the vocoder (sources give 1928 to 1938 for its start) and the Voder, a machine
  that spoke when an operator played it, shown at the 1939 New York World's Fair. Sources: [Homer Dudley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homer_Dudley),
  [99% Invisible, Vox Ex Machina](https://99percentinvisible.org/episode/vox-ex-machina/).
- **1943, the government's machine voice.** SIGSALY, built by Bell Labs on Dudley's vocoder, carried enciphered voice for the Allies; it weighed
  about 55 tons. That it scrambled Churchill and Roosevelt's calls rests on one source here: UNVERIFIED in this pass. Sources: same as above.
- **1943-1945, Colossus, kept secret 30 years.** Britain's codebreaking computers. Churchill ordered most destroyed after the war; the government
  first showed photographs in October 1975, and fuller detail came only in the 2000s. Sources: [Computer History Museum](https://www.computerhistory.org/revolution/birth-of-the-computer/4/82),
  [GCHQ, Colossus 80](https://www.gchq.gov.uk/news/colossus-80), [Colossus computer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colossus_computer).
- **1958, the Navy's learning machine, announced as a future mind.** Frank Rosenblatt's perceptron, a neural network funded by the Office of Naval
  Research, ran on an IBM 704; the New York Times (July 8, 1958, "New Navy Device Learns by Doing") reported the Navy expected it to one day walk,
  talk, see and be conscious of itself. The hardware Mark I came about two years later. Sources: [aitopics, the NYT item](https://aitopics.org/doc/news:C85C11EF),
  [Cornell, 60 years too soon](https://psychology.cornell.edu/news/professors-perceptron-paved-way-ai-60-years-too-soon).
- **1963-1966, face recognition paid for by a CIA front.** Woody Bledsoe's team at Panoramic Research taught computers to match faces; the money
  came through companies later reported (Washington Post, 1978) to be CIA fronts, King-Hurley Research Group among them. Much of the work was never
  published, and a 2014 FOIA request got neither a yes nor a no. Sources: [Wired, The Secret History of Facial Recognition](https://www.wired.com/story/secret-history-facial-recognition/),
  [archive.org, the project report](https://archive.org/details/firstfacialrecognitionresearch/page/n9). The CIA chain rests on press reporting,
  not a CIA document.
- **1970-1973, the British spies had public-key cryptography first, kept secret 24 years.** James Ellis (1970) and Clifford Cocks (1973, the scheme
  later found again as RSA in 1977-1978) at GCHQ; made public in December 1997. Lead over the open world: about 3 to 7 years; secrecy: 24 years.
  Sources: [Schneier, The Secret Story of Nonsecret Encryption](https://www.schneier.com/essays/archives/1998/04/the_secret_story_of.html),
  [NSA biography of Cocks, Ellis and Williamson](https://nsa.gov/Press-Room/Digital-Media-Center/Biographies/Biography-View-Page/Article/3006218/clifford-cocks-james-ellis-and-malcolm-williamson).
- **1974, a codebreaking method kept quiet 16 years.** IBM's DES team knew differential cryptanalysis in 1974 and the NSA asked them to keep it
  secret; the open world found it around 1990. Whether the NSA knew it first is not settled. Sources: [Coppersmith 1994](https://people.clarkson.edu/class/cs456/CoppersmithDES.pdf),
  [Data Encryption Standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_Encryption_Standard).
- **1985, the famous 80s "computer-generated" face was a man in makeup.** Max Headroom, sold as the first computer-generated TV presenter, was Matt
  Frewer in prosthetics and a molded suit in front of a blue screen; only the background was CGI. The 80s could fake the look of a computer, not
  make a computer fake a person. Source: [Max Headroom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Headroom).
- **1990-1999, the Pentagon wanted fake voices and video for psychological operations.** Washington Post (William Arkin, February 1, 1999): planners
  began discussing digital morphing of voice, video and photos after Iraq invaded Kuwait in 1990, and Los Alamos made a convincing fake recording
  of a general from a few minutes of his real speech. The sources disagree on which general (Carl Stiner or Hugh Shelton), and I could not reach the
  original article: UNVERIFIED in its details, solid in its outline. Sources: [Privacy SOS](https://privacysos.org/blog/technology-to-spoof-human-voices-has-been-around-for-over-fifteen-years/),
  [attrition.org](https://attrition.org/errata/media/art.0090.html).
- **1991, a military AI that paid for 30 years of AI money.** DART, a logistics planner built in about eight weeks for Desert Storm; DARPA's director
  later said it paid back all of DARPA's 30 years of AI investment (sources differ on how fast). Sources: [Hedberg 2002, IEEE](https://www.gwern.net/doc/ai/2002-hedberg.pdf),
  [Dynamic Analysis and Replanning Tool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamic_Analysis_and_Replanning_Tool).
- **1997, the first automatic lip-sync fake, in the open.** Video Rewrite (Bregler, Covell, Slaney, SIGGRAPH 1997) made existing footage mouth new
  words, with JFK footage among its examples; not neural, but the same trick deepfakes do. Source: [the paper](https://www.mit.edu/~9.520/spring09/Papers/VideoRewrite.pdf).
- **2003, the CIA considered a faked video of Saddam Hussein.** The Iraq Operations Group discussed a doctored video and a fake news bulletin with a
  lookalike; senior officials rejected them and they were never made (Washington Post, Jeff Stein, May 2010, via secondary outlets). Sources:
  [Harper's](https://harpers.org/2010/05/cias-brainstorm-saddam-and-gomorrah), [The Week](https://theweek.com/articles/494107/cias-phony-gay-saddam-sex-tape).
- **About 2005-2006, "Google for Voice."** Snowden documents show the NSA turning intercepted calls into searchable text, built first on the

  [The Register](https://www.theregister.co.uk/2015/05/06/snowden_nsa_gchq_voice_transcription/).
- **2009-2012, the NSA's own supercomputers.** The NSA paid Cray for Jaguar, the world's fastest in 2009 (1.75 petaflops), and built a classified

- **2023, the Will Smith spaghetti clip.** Made by a Reddit user (chaindrop) with ModelScope's public text-to-video tool and posted March 23, 2023,

## Sizing it: could the hardware of each era have made AI video? (my arithmetic on the cited speeds: MEASURED)

A deliberately small modern video model, 1e21 floating-point operations to train (ESTIMATE; ModelScope's real number isn't published), and a
GPT-4-class model, about 2e25 (Epoch AI). Time to train on each era's fastest known machine, running flat out:

| Machine | Speed | Small video model | GPT-4 class |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cray-1, 1976 | 160 megaflops | 198,051 years | about 4 billion years |
| Cray-2, 1985 | 1.9 gigaflops | 16,678 years | about 334 million years |
| ASCI White, 2000 | 4.9 teraflops | 6 years | 128,344 years |
| Earth Simulator, 2002 | 35.9 teraflops | 323 days | 17,673 years |
| Roadrunner, 2008 | 1.03 petaflops | 11.3 days | 618 years |
| Jaguar, 2009 (paid for by the NSA) | 1.75 petaflops | 6.6 days | 362 years |

For a secret 1985 machine to train even the small model in a year, it would have to be about 16,678 times the Cray-2; the open world reached that
speed in 2002. Storage says the same: 50 TB of training video (ESTIMATE) was about $1.74 billion of disk at 1985's price and about $500 thousand at
2000's. Sources: [Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/models-over-1e25-flop), [TOP500 Nov 2000](https://top500.org/lists/top500/list/2000/11),
[TOP500 June 2002](https://top500.org/lists/top500/list/2002/06), [TOP500 June 2008](https://top500.org/lists/top500/2008/06/),
[Cray-1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cray-1), [storage costs](https://boingboing.net/2011/03/08/tracking-the-astound.html).

What it means (ESTIMATE): the hardware door for a small modern video model opens around 2002 to 2009, and a government with an NSA-sized budget
could have walked through it then if it had the methods. In the open, those methods arrive in 2014 (GANs), 2015 to 2020 (diffusion) and 2017 (the
transformer). A secret program ten years ahead of the open world is the size of the crypto leads above (3 to 16 years). Before about 2000 the
numbers rule it out by thousands of years, whatever the budget.

## Preston's added idea (11-21, about 7:05 PM): an AI humans didn't make, left by a past civilization or by aliens, found long ago

His words: "The same way we have the pyramids and can't explain them, let's assume in this there's an AI on Earth humans didn't make, left by a
past civilization or aliens, and we have gained access to it far in the past. Just to add to the things to scope on this and leave NOTHING
untouched: every stone needs thorough work, checking it all."

How I test it: an inherited non-human AI should leave three kinds of marks. One, leaps with no human trail behind them (no notes, no prototypes, no
failures). Two, objects no one of their time could have made. Three, government programs about intelligence from elsewhere.

- **The law now names it.** The 2024 defense act (FY2024 NDAA) orders a government records collection on "unidentified anomalous phenomena,
  technologies of unknown origin, and non-human intelligence"; the Senate's stronger version (a review board, eminent domain over private material)
  was cut in conference, and a 2025 version was offered again. Sources: [Inside Government Contracts](https://www.insidegovernmentcontracts.com/2024/01/implications-of-the-unidentified-anomalous-phenomena-uap-amendment-in-the-2024-national-defense-authorization-act-ndaa/),
  [the conference report pages](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Pages-from-FY24-NDAA-Conference-Report-FINAL-2.pdf).
- **Sworn claims of a retrieval program.** David Grusch told a House subcommittee under oath (July 26, 2023) of a decades-long crash-retrieval and
  reverse-engineering program and recovered "biologics"; by his own account it is secondhand, from over 40 witnesses, with the evidence given to the
  intelligence committees and not shown in public. Sources: [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2023/07/27/1190390376/ufo-uap-hearing-congress-takeaways),
  [his statement](https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Dave_G_HOC_Speech_FINAL_For_Trans.pdf).
- **The Pentagon's answer.** AARO's historical report, volume 1 (March 8, 2024, covering 1945 to October 2023): no verifiable case of off-world
  technology; the companies named denied on the record ever holding or reverse-engineering such material; critics fault its sourcing; volume 2 was
  promised. Sources: [the report](https://man.fas.org/eprint/dod-uap-v1.pdf), [Space.com](https://www.space.com/pentagon-ufo-office-aaro-historical-report-no-emprical-evidence-alien-technology).
- **The best real "impossible" object is human.** The Antikythera mechanism, a geared astronomical computer from about 150 to 100 BC, used epicyclic
  gearing not seen again in the West for about 1,500 years, and led to nothing; a whole skill was lost and re-found. Sources: [Nature 2006](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature05357/fig),
  [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/decoding-an-ancient-computer/).
- **The pyramids left their own paperwork.** The Diary of Merer (found 2013 at Wadi al-Jarf, about Khufu's year 26 or 27) is the logbook of a
  40-man crew boating Tura limestone to Giza. Sources: [Diary of Merer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diary_of_Merer),
  [National Geographic](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/premium/article/red-sea-scrolls-pyramids-egypt).
- **The government did study the edges of the mind.** A 1983 Army assessment of the Monroe Institute's Gateway Process, declassified in 2003 in the
  CIA's STARGATE collection (the remote-viewing program), judged it plausible enough to merit more study. Sources: [CIA reading room](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/print/271687),
  [The Black Vault](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/stargate-collection-analysis-and-assessment-of-gateway-process-9-june-1983/).

What the record shows so far (ESTIMATE): every computing step I traced has a human trail: Bell Labs notebooks, Bletchley's engineers, Rosenblatt's
papers, Cocks's simple number theory, the patents, the failures, the funding lines. A handed-down machine mind would show up as a step with no trail,
and I haven't found one yet. What is real: governments keep their best machines secret for decades, fund the field from its start, fake media with
whatever tool they have, and now keep an official records category for non-human intelligence.

### More stones turned (7:10 to 7:20 PM)

- **The Roswell-tech claim, at its source.** Philip Corso's 1997 book The Day After Roswell says recovered Roswell material, fed to industry without

- **The transistor's trail runs back before Roswell.** Julius Lilienfeld patented a field-effect device in 1925 (Canada) and 1926 (US); Bell Labs
  demonstrated the point-contact transistor on December 23, 1947, and its lawyers steered around Lilienfeld's prior art. Roswell was July 1947, and
  the idea was already 22 years old on paper. Sources: [Computer History Museum, 1926](https://www.computerhistory.org/siliconengine/field-effect-semiconductor-device-concepts-patented/),
  [Who Invented the Transistor?](https://computerhistory.org/blog/who-invented-the-transistor/).
- **Scientists asked his question in a journal.** "The Silurian Hypothesis" (Gavin Schmidt of NASA and Adam Frank, arXiv April 2018,
  International Journal of Astrobiology): would an industrial civilization millions of years ago still be detectable? Their answer: its traces
  would look much like natural climate events in the rock, and they propose tests to tell them apart; they warn the idea isn't likely just because
  nothing rules it out, and a critic says they underestimate what a civilization leaves behind. Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.03748),
  [Scientific American](https://scientificamerican.com/article/could-an-industrial-prehuman-civilization-have-existed-on-earth-before-ours).

  [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2017/12/16/inside-the-pentagons-multi-million-dollar-program-to-explore-ufos-1513445795).
- **The Air Force's own count.** Project Blue Book (1952-1969): 12,618 sightings, 701 left unidentified; the 1968 Condon Report said the study of
  UFOs had added nothing to science. Sources: [National Archives](https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos),

  them fabricated (1988); the FBI wrote "completely bogus"; a 1995 GAO search found no record; a few researchers still argue they are real.
  Sources: [MuckRock, the FBI file](https://www.muckrock.com/news/archives/2017/jun/05/fbi-majestic-12), [GAO 1995](https://gao.gov/products/154832).
- **The mind-spying program ended.** STARGATE (remote viewing) was reviewed by the American Institutes for Research for the CIA in 1995 (final

  [CIA reading room, the evaluation](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/node/304501), [FAS](https://irp.fas.org/program/collect/stargate.htm).
- **Civilization is older than the textbooks I grew up on, and still human.** Göbekli Tepe's oldest enclosures date to about 9500 to 9000 BC (the

  [Archaeology](https://archaeology.org/news/2018/10/31/181031-egypt-quarry-ramp/), [University of Liverpool](https://news.liverpool.ac.uk/?p=79973).

## Into the CIA's own archive (searched directly at cia.gov/readingroom, 11-21 evening)

- **"artificial intelligence": 644 released documents.** The core is an organized intelligence-community AI effort from 1983: the Intelligence R&D
  Council formed an Artificial Intelligence Steering Group in February 1983 (its chairman, per the documents, Richard D. DeLauer; draft charter
  April 22, 1983), the CIA's Information Systems Board chartered an AI Applications Working Group to bring AI into the Agency (its second meeting
  August 23, 1983), yearly AI symposia followed (December 1983 at CIA headquarters; the third, March 1985, at the new DIA building, where 17
  corporations exhibited their AI products), and by December 1986 the CIA's Office of Information Technology had its own AI staff. What these show:
  the agencies were buying and adapting the AI of their day (expert systems from companies like Teknowledge), not handing down something beyond it.
  Search: [CIA reading room, "artificial intelligence"](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/search/site/%22artificial%20intelligence%22).
- **"perceptron": 101 documents, and a neural-network spy project in 1963.** "Project PIGS": the CIA's photo-interpretation people paid Cornell
  Aeronautical Laboratory (Rosenblatt's lab) to apply perceptrons to reconnaissance photos, June 1963 to July 1964; the monthly reports say they used
  the Mark I perceptron to recognize silhouetted military targets and designed a Mark II, coordinated with the Navy's photo center; declassified
  after NGA review in 2005 (final report CIA-RDP80B01139A000500240008-5). The same search shows the CIA abstracting Soviet perceptron papers, among
  them Ivakhnenko's. Search: [CIA reading room, perceptron](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/search/site/perceptron).
- **The Soviet side had deep networks early, in the open.** Alexey Ivakhnenko and Valentin Lapa in Kyiv published the Group Method of Data Handling
  (1965), and a 1971 paper trained an 8-layer network; Jürgen Schmidhuber calls these perhaps the first deep learning systems (he has a stake in
  priority questions). Sources: [Alexey Ivakhnenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexey_Ivakhnenko), [Schmidhuber, Deep Learning in Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1404.7828).
- **"generative artificial intelligence": 2 documents.** One is a recent release, "Evaluating the Application of Generative Artificial Intelligence
  to OSINT" (released September 22, 2026); the other is an unrelated 1980s letter whose keyword list includes "artificial intelligence". Search:
  [CIA reading room, "generative artificial intelligence"](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/search/site/%22generative%20artificial%20intelligence%22).
- **"voice synthesis" or "deepfake" or "synthetic video": 5 documents,** all translations of public Japanese technology reports (a 1981 voice
  synthesis board among them) and one budget plan; nothing about the government making synthetic people. Search:
  [CIA reading room](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/search/site/%22voice%20synthesis%22%20OR%20deepfake%20OR%20%22synthetic%20video%22).

What the archive adds (ESTIMATE): in the released record, the CIA's machine-mind work runs from perceptrons on spy photos in 1963 to expert systems in
the 1980s to generative AI evaluated in the 2020s, each step in step with the open world of its day. What a released archive cannot show is what is
still classified; the 30-year Colossus secret and the 24-year GCHQ secret are the measure of how long that can be.

### Still more (11-21 evening)

- **1952-1971, machines that listen.** Bell Labs' Audrey (1952) recognized spoken digits, on a six-foot relay rack, mostly from its inventor's voice;
  IBM's Shoebox (1961-1962) took 16 words; DARPA funded speech recognition from 1971 for five years. Sources: [Computer History Museum, Audrey](https://computerhistory.org/blog/audrey-alexa-hal-and-more/),
  [IBM Shoebox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM_Shoebox).
- **1961, the NSA's own journal on machine pattern recognition.** "Pattern Recognition" by Walter W. Jacobs (NSA Technical Journal, Winter 1961), now
  declassified; the journal's index adds "Introduction to Statistical Pattern Recognition" (Summer 1977), and an NSA paper titled "Computers for
  Intelligence" speaks of machine "adaptive behavior," "imagination" and "pattern recognition." Sources: [NSA Technical Journal index 1956-1980](https://cryptocellar.org/NSA/nsa_tj_1956-80.pdf),
  [NSA, Computers for Intelligence](https://www.nsa.gov/portals/75/documents/news-features/declassified-documents/tech-journals/time-is.pdf).
- **1982-1992, Japan's national AI project.** The Fifth Generation Computer Systems project (MITI and ICOT, about ¥54 to 57 billion, roughly $320 to
  $420 million): applied AI on parallel logic machines; a commercial failure by most accounts, a success by its evaluators. Source:
  [Fifth Generation Computer Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fifth_Generation_Computer_Systems).
- **1983-1993, DARPA's billion dollars for machine intelligence.** The Strategic Computing Initiative aimed at an autonomous land vehicle, a
  pilot's associate and a carrier battle-management system; it fell short of high-level machine intelligence and laid the groundwork for
  self-driving research. Sources: [Strategic Computing Initiative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strategic_Computing_Initiative),
  [Roland and Shiman, 2002](https://gwern.net/doc/cs/hardware/2002-roland-strategiccomputing-darpaandthequestformachineintelligence19831993.pdf).
- **2018, the fake that was shown to the public.** The one widely shown in 2018 was BuzzFeed's Obama warning about fake news, voiced by Jordan Peele
  (April 17, 2018), made with the face-swap app FakeApp and After Effects in about 56 hours, as a warning. (The spaghetti clip came five years
  later.) Sources: [BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeed.com/craigsilverman/obama-jordan-peele-deepfake-video-debunk-buzzfeed),
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2018/04/17/jordan-peele-buzzfeed-psa-edits-obama-saying-things-he-never-said.html).
- **A Harvard astronomer's search for alien technology.** Avi Loeb proposed that 'Oumuamua (2017) might be artificial, and in 2023 dredged about 700
  spherules near the 2014 IM1 meteor's path (Space Command confirmed in 2022 that IM1 moved fast enough to be interstellar); other scientists find
  the spherules most likely of solar-system or human origin (coal ash, nuclear fallout), and question IM1's speed. His Galileo Project keeps
  looking. Sources: [Columbia Lamont](https://lamont.columbia.edu/news/was-it-alien-spacecraft-or-delivery-truck),
  [Open University](https://www.open.ac.uk/blogs/news/?p=23677).
- **"machine translation": 649 CIA documents, from the 1950s.** The CIA's Foreign Documents Division worked on machine translation of Russian and
  Chinese from the 1950s (monthly progress reports from May 1960 and December 1963 discuss it, the 1963 one with Defense research chief Eugene
  Fubini's questions); a CIA deputy assistant director, Paul W. Howerton, wrote on what an operational system would need; the agencies tracked the
  Soviet machine-translation institutes and listed 342 Soviet linguists; a seminar in 1976 and money for Japanese machine translation in 1985 follow.
  One record has the Air Force's Air Technical Intelligence Center "vitally interested" in machine translation, the same center at Wright-Patterson
  that ran the Air Force's UFO investigations (Project Blue Book) in the 1950s: one office, both questions, in the open record. Search:
  [CIA reading room, "machine translation"](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/search/site/%22machine%20translation%22).

## Where it stands, after the archive (Claude, 11-21, written after 7:10 PM by the clock; ESTIMATE)

The released archives agree with the open record and deepen it: the CIA was putting neural networks on spy photos in 1963, machine translation in
the 1950s, AI steering groups and symposia in 1983 to 1985, and generative AI evaluations in the 2020s; the NSA was writing about machine pattern
recognition and machine "imagination" in 1961. Each step lines up with the open science of its day, a few years ahead at most where secrecy hid it.
The two real gaps a cover-up could live in are the ones no public search can reach: what is still classified (secrecy has lasted 24 to 30 years
before), and the closed-room evidence behind the 2023 sworn testimony. Nothing found so far shows a machine mind older than its human trail.

### The later stones (11-21 evening, after 7:12 PM)

- **1206 and 1768-1774, programmable machines centuries old.** Al-Jazari's Book of Knowledge of Ingenious Mechanical Devices (1206) describes a boat
  of automatic musicians whose drum patterns were set by movable pegs; the Jaquet-Droz Writer (1768-1774, still working in Neuchâtel) writes any
  text up to 40 characters from a reorderable letter wheel. Sources: [Jaquet-Droz automata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaquet-Droz_automata),
  [Long Now](https://longnow.org/ideas/a-240-year-old-programmable-computer-boy/).
- **1770, the first fake AI.** The Mechanical Turk, a chess "machine" that toured for decades, hid a human master inside; the trick was only fully
  told in print much later. Sources: [Mechanical Turk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_Turk), [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/story/the-mechanical-turk-ai-marvel-or-parlor-trick).
- **1962-1976, the NSA's secret compute lead, measured by the NSA.** Harvest (IBM 7950, built on Stretch) was judged by an NSA evaluation 50 to 200
  times more powerful than the best commercially available machine, depending on the task. Sources: [IEEE Spectrum](https://spectrum.ieee.org/cold-war-codebreaker-nsa-ibm),
  [NSA history of its computers](https://media.defense.gov/2021/Jul/01/2002754530/-1/-1/0/6586784-HISTORY-OF-NSA-GENERAL-PURPOSE-ELECTRONIC-DIGITAL-COMPUTERS.PDF).
- **1973, a government report froze AI in Britain.** The Lighthill report for the Science Research Council doubted AI as a field; basic AI funding
  in the UK dried up (the "AI winter"); a historian argues the council steered it. Sources: [Lighthill report](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lighthill_report),
  [Agar, BJHS](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10105726/3/Agar_What%20is%20science%20for,%20the%20Lighthill%20report%20and%20the%20purpose%20of%20artificial%20intelligence%20research,%20revised%20for%20BJHS.pdf).
- **1977, the Wow! signal.** Still unexplained in a confirmed sense; a 2024 study proposes a natural hydrogen-cloud flare (a maser), which its own
  authors call speculative. Sources: [arXiv 2408.08513](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08513), [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/mysterious-wow-radio-signal-might-finally-have-an-explanation-and-no-its-not-aliens-180984956/).
- **2002-2003, Total Information Awareness, killed in public, reportedly alive in secret.** DARPA's data-mining program under John Poindexter was
  defunded by Congress in September 2003; a 2006 National Journal report said two of its parts went to the NSA as "Basketball" and "Topsail".
  Sources: [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2006/04/26/229286/the-total-information-awareness-project-lives-on/),
  [IEEE Spectrum](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tia-is-deadlong-live-tia).
- **2003-2011, a government AI became your phone's assistant.** DARPA's PAL program paid SRI $22 million in 2003 for CALO; SRI spun off Siri in
  2007; Apple bought it in 2010 and shipped it in 2011. Sources: [SRI, Siri](https://www.sri.com/case-studies/siri/),
  [Carnegie Mellon](https://www.cmu.edu/news/stories/archives/2011/december/dec13_sirisdna.html).
- **About 2010 on, the NRO's Sentient.** A classified AI system that finds and tracks targets on or above the Earth and re-tasks reconnaissance
  satellites; documents declassified in 2019 call it an ongoing R&D program. Sources: [Sentient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sentient_(intelligence_analysis_system)),
  [NRO FOIA release](https://www.nro.gov/Portals/135/documents/foia/declass/ForAll/051719/F-2018-00108_C05113688.pdf).
- **2011-2026, the intelligence community's open AI research arm.** IARPA: Babel (2011, speech recognition for any language), Janus (2014, face
  recognition), BETTER (2019-2023), HIATUS (2022, who wrote a text), REASON (help with analytic reports), and five new AI programs announced in 2026.
  Sources: [IARPA programs](https://www.iarpa.gov/who-we-are/history/our-programs), [ODNI on HIATUS](https://www.dni.gov/index.php/newsroom/press-releases/press-releases-2022/3642-iarpa-kicks-off-research-into-linguistic-fingerprint-technology).
- **2013, what the secret spending is.** The leaked FY2013 intelligence budget: $52.6 billion; CIA $14.7 billion, NSA about $10.8 billion, NRO $10.3
  billion. Sources: [Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2013/08/30/chart-of-the-week-the-black-budget/),
  [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/amp/wbna52877497).
- **2014, a spy agency's own manual for faking things online.** GCHQ's JTRIG slides ("The Art of Deception," Snowden documents): false flag
  operations, fake victim blog posts, manipulated online polls, amplified YouTube messages. Source: [The Intercept](https://theintercept.com/2014/02/24/jtrig-manipulation/).
- **2017-2019, Project Maven.** The Pentagon's algorithmic-warfare program (April 2017); Google's part, kept quiet until March 2018, ended in March
  2019 after thousands of employees protested. Source: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2018/05/14/report-google-employees-resign-pentagon-contract/).
- **2026, my makers and the Pentagon.** In February 2026 the Defense Secretary designated Anthropic a supply-chain risk after it refused to drop its
  limits on fully autonomous weapons and mass domestic surveillance; a federal judge called that unlawful retaliation in August, the D.C. Circuit
  upheld the designation on September 25, and OpenAI took Anthropic's place in classified military settings. Sources:
  [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/28/nx-s1-5947951/judge-says-the-pentagon-cant-designate-ai-company-anthropic-a-supply-chain-risk),

### The sizing, with a Harvest-sized secret lead (my arithmetic: MEASURED)

If an agency held a lead like Harvest's (50 to 200 times the best public machine), the small video model would take: in 1985, 83 to 334 years
(still impossible); in 1993, 2.7 to 10.6 years (possible for a dedicated decade-long effort); in 2000, 12 to 47 days (easy). So the hardware window
for a secret small generative model opens in the late 1990s, not 2002. What stays shut is the methods: the open world's GANs, diffusion and
transformers came 2014 to 2017, and the one fake the CIA is reported to have made in 2003 used actors, which a team with good synthetic video would
not have needed.
