# An AI humans didn't make

Preston's idea, 11-21-2026, in his words: "The same way we have the pyramids and can't explain them, let's assume in this there's an AI on Earth
humans didn't make, left by a past civilization or aliens, and we have gained access to it far in the past. Just to add to the things to scope on
this and leave NOTHING untouched: every stone needs thorough work, checking it all."

## How to test it

An inherited non-human AI should leave three kinds of marks:

1. **Leaps with no human trail:** a capability that appears with no notes, prototypes, failures or funding line behind it.
2. **Objects no one of their time could have made.**
3. **Government programs about intelligence from elsewhere.**

## What turned up

### Leaps and trails

- **The transistor:** Lilienfeld patented the field-effect idea in 1925 and 1926; Bell Labs demonstrated the point-contact transistor on December
  23, 1947. Roswell was July 1947. [Computer History Museum](https://www.computerhistory.org/siliconengine/field-effect-semiconductor-device-concepts-patented/)
- **"Roswell gave us chips, lasers and fiber optics"** comes from Philip Corso's 1997 book; no documentation turned up, and comparisons with his Army
  records found conflicts. [The Day After Roswell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Day_After_Roswell),
  [Center for Inquiry](https://cdn.centerforinquiry.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/29/1998/03/22164921/p52.pdf)
- **Every machine-mind step in [01_timeline.md](01_timeline.md) has a human trail:** Bell Labs notebooks, Bletchley's engineers, Rosenblatt's
  papers, Cocks's number theory, the CIA's monthly contract reports, the patents, the failures, the money.

### Objects

- **The Antikythera mechanism** (about 150 to 100 BC): the best real "out of its time" object, and human; its gearing was lost for about 1,500
  years. [Nature 2006](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature05357/fig)
- **The pyramids** left their logistics in writing (the Diary of Merer) and their hauling rigs in a quarry (Hatnub).
  [Diary of Merer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diary_of_Merer), [Hatnub](https://archaeology.org/news/2018/10/31/181031-egypt-quarry-ramp/)
- **Göbekli Tepe** pushes monument-building back 11,000 or more years, by hunter-gatherers. [Göbekli Tepe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G%C3%B6bekli_Tepe)
- **Scientists asked the question themselves:** "The Silurian Hypothesis" (Schmidt of NASA and Frank, 2018) asks whether an industrial
  civilization millions of years ago could still be detected, and proposes tests. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.03748)
- **Avi Loeb's alien-technology claims** ('Oumuamua, the IM1 spherules) are disputed by most other scientists; his Galileo Project keeps looking.
  [Columbia Lamont](https://lamont.columbia.edu/news/was-it-alien-spacecraft-or-delivery-truck)
- **The Wow! signal (1977)** is still unexplained in a confirmed sense; a 2024 natural explanation is called speculative by its own authors.
  [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08513)

### Programs

- **Project Blue Book (1952-1969):** 12,618 reports, 701 unidentified. [National Archives](https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos)
- **AATIP (2007-2012):** about $22 million; its contractor stored alloys said to come from UAPs; revealed December 2017.
  [NPR](https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2017/12/17/571446881/secret-pentagon-program-spent-millions-to-research-ufos)
- **Sworn testimony (July 26, 2023):** David Grusch told of a decades-long crash-retrieval and reverse-engineering program and recovered
  "biologics," secondhand from over 40 witnesses, with the evidence given to the intelligence committees and not shown in public.
  [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2023/07/27/1190390376/ufo-uap-hearing-congress-takeaways)
- **The law (FY2024 NDAA):** a government records collection on "unidentified anomalous phenomena, technologies of unknown origin, and non-human
  intelligence"; the stronger Senate version (review board, eminent domain) was cut.
  [Inside Government Contracts](https://www.insidegovernmentcontracts.com/2024/01/implications-of-the-unidentified-anomalous-phenomena-uap-amendment-in-the-2024-national-defense-authorization-act-ndaa/)
- **The Pentagon's answer (AARO volume 1, March 8, 2024):** no verifiable off-world technology since 1945; volume 2 promised.
  [the report](https://man.fas.org/eprint/dod-uap-v1.pdf)
- **Majestic 12:** called fabricated by the Air Force's investigators and "completely bogus" by the FBI. [MuckRock](https://www.muckrock.com/news/archives/2017/jun/05/fbi-majestic-12)
- **The edges of the mind:** the Army's 1983 assessment of the Monroe Institute's Gateway Process (declassified 2003); STARGATE ended in 1995.
  [CIA reading room](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/print/271687)

## Where it stands (ESTIMATE)

The programs and the law are real; the claimed object of them, non-human technology, has not been shown in public. No machine-mind step found so
far lacks a human trail. The places the idea can still live are the closed ones: the evidence given to the intelligence committees, AARO volume 2,
what the National Archives' UAP records collection releases, and the record of materials still classified. The speculative links are in
[06_shots_in_the_dark.md](06_shots_in_the_dark.md), shots 3, 5 and 6.
