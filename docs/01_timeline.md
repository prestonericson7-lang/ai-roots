# Timeline: thinking machines, secret machines and faked media, oldest first

Labels: **UNVERIFIED** means one secondary source only or details in dispute. Times and numbers come from the sources linked on each line.

## Before the electronic age

- **About 9500 to 9000 BC: Göbekli Tepe.** The oldest known monumental site, built by hunter-gatherers (the excavators say 11,000 BC or earlier;
  about 5 percent is dug). Civilization is older than the old textbooks, and still human. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G%C3%B6bekli_Tepe),
  [Archaeology](https://archaeology.org/issues/422-2105/features/9591-turkey-gobekli-tepe-hunter-gatherers)
- **About 2560 BC: the pyramids' paperwork.** The Diary of Merer (found 2013 at Wadi al-Jarf), the logbook of a 40-man crew boating Tura limestone
  to Giza in Khufu's year 26 or 27; at Khufu's Hatnub quarry, a steep ramp with post holes for a rope hauling rig (announced 2018).
  [Diary of Merer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diary_of_Merer), [Hatnub ramp](https://archaeology.org/news/2018/10/31/181031-egypt-quarry-ramp/)
- **About 150 to 100 BC: the Antikythera mechanism.** A geared astronomical computer whose epicyclic gearing is not seen again in the West for about
  1,500 years. [Nature 2006](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature05357/fig), [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/decoding-an-ancient-computer/)
- **1206: Al-Jazari.** Programmable automatic musicians, the drum patterns set by movable pegs. [Jaquet-Droz automata (background)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaquet-Droz_automata)
- **1768 to 1774: the Jaquet-Droz Writer.** A doll that writes any text up to 40 characters from a reorderable letter wheel; still working.
  [Long Now](https://longnow.org/ideas/a-240-year-old-programmable-computer-boy/)
- **1770: the Mechanical Turk.** The first fake AI: a chess "machine" with a human master hidden inside. [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/story/the-mechanical-turk-ai-marvel-or-parlor-trick)

## 1925 to 1959: war machines and the first machine minds

- **1925 to 1926: the transistor on paper.** Julius Lilienfeld patents a field-effect device in Canada and the US.
  [Computer History Museum](https://www.computerhistory.org/siliconengine/field-effect-semiconductor-device-concepts-patented/)
- **1937 to 1940: a man erased.** Secret-police chief Nikolai Yezhov, executed in February 1940, is painted out of a 1937 photo with Stalin.
  [History.com](https://www.history.com/news/josef-stalin-great-purge-photo-retouching)
- **1938 to 1939: machine speech.** Homer Dudley's vocoder and the Voder, a talking machine shown at the 1939 New York World's Fair.
  [Homer Dudley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homer_Dudley), [99% Invisible](https://99percentinvisible.org/episode/vox-ex-machina/)
- **1943: the government's machine voice.** SIGSALY, built on the vocoder, carries enciphered Allied speech; about 55 tons. That it carried
  Churchill and Roosevelt's calls rests on one source here: UNVERIFIED.
- **1943 to 1945: Colossus, secret for 30 years.** Britain's codebreaking computers; Churchill ordered most destroyed; first photographs released in
  October 1975. [Computer History Museum](https://www.computerhistory.org/revolution/birth-of-the-computer/4/82), [GCHQ](https://www.gchq.gov.uk/news/colossus-80)
- **July 1947: Roswell. December 23, 1947: the transistor at Bell Labs,** whose lawyers had to steer around Lilienfeld's 22-year-old patents.
  [Who invented the transistor?](https://computerhistory.org/blog/who-invented-the-transistor/)
- **1950s: the CIA starts on machine translation** of Russian and Chinese; the Air Force's Air Technical Intelligence Center, which also ran the UFO
  investigations, is recorded as "vitally interested". [CIA reading room](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/search/site/%22machine%20translation%22)
- **1952: Audrey.** Bell Labs' machine recognizes spoken digits. [Computer History Museum](https://computerhistory.org/blog/audrey-alexa-hal-and-more/)
- **1952 to 1969: Project Blue Book.** 12,618 UFO reports, 701 left unidentified; the 1968 Condon Report says the study added nothing to science.
  [National Archives](https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos)
- **1956: the Dartmouth meeting names the field** (privately funded).
- **July 8, 1958: the Navy's learning machine.** The New York Times reports the Navy expects Rosenblatt's perceptron to one day walk, talk, see and
  be conscious of itself. [aitopics](https://aitopics.org/doc/news:C85C11EF), [Cornell](https://psychology.cornell.edu/news/professors-perceptron-paved-way-ai-60-years-too-soon)

## 1960 to 1979: the agencies' own machine minds

- **1961: the NSA writes on machine pattern recognition** (Walter W. Jacobs, NSA Technical Journal; an NSA paper speaks of machine "imagination").
  [index](https://cryptocellar.org/NSA/nsa_tj_1956-80.pdf), [Computers for Intelligence](https://www.nsa.gov/portals/75/documents/news-features/declassified-documents/tech-journals/time-is.pdf)
- **1961 to 1962: IBM Shoebox,** 16 spoken words. [IBM Shoebox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM_Shoebox)
- **1962 on: ARPA funds AI** (Licklider's office: Project MAC at MIT, a matching project at Stanford); one history says its AI spending exceeded the
  rest of the world's combined. [DARPA](https://www.darpa.mil/about/innovation-timeline/project-mac), [National Academies](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/6323/chapter/11)
- **1962 to 1976: Harvest.** The NSA's codebreaker, judged by the NSA 50 to 200 times more powerful than the best commercial machine.
  [IEEE Spectrum](https://spectrum.ieee.org/cold-war-codebreaker-nsa-ibm)
- **1963 to 1964: Project PIGS.** The CIA pays Rosenblatt's lab to put perceptrons on spy photos; the Mark I recognizes silhouetted military targets;
  a Mark II is designed. Declassified 2005. [CIA reading room](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/search/site/perceptron)
- **1963 to 1966: face recognition through CIA fronts** (Woody Bledsoe, Panoramic Research). [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/secret-history-facial-recognition/)
- **1965 and 1971: deep networks in Kyiv.** Ivakhnenko and Lapa's Group Method of Data Handling; an 8-layer network in 1971.
  [Alexey Ivakhnenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexey_Ivakhnenko)
- **1970 to 1973: public-key cryptography at GCHQ,** secret until December 1997 (24 years). [Schneier](https://www.schneier.com/essays/archives/1998/04/the_secret_story_of.html)
- **1971: DARPA funds speech recognition** for five years.
- **1973: the Lighthill report** freezes basic AI money in Britain. [Lighthill report](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lighthill_report)
- **1974: a codebreaking method kept quiet 16 years** (differential cryptanalysis, IBM and the NSA, found in the open about 1990).
  [Coppersmith 1994](https://people.clarkson.edu/class/cs456/CoppersmithDES.pdf)
- **1976: the Cray-1,** 160 megaflops; the NSA may have been its real first customer. [Cray-1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cray-1)
- **1977: the Wow! signal;** the NSA's journal runs "Introduction to Statistical Pattern Recognition". [arXiv 2408.08513](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08513)

## 1980 to 1999: AI programs, psychic spies and the first fakes of voice and face

- **1982 to 1992: Japan's Fifth Generation project,** about ¥54 to 57 billion. [Fifth Generation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fifth_Generation_Computer_Systems)
- **February 1983: the intelligence community's AI Steering Group;** the CIA's AI Applications Working Group; AI symposia in 1983 to 1985 where
  companies show their products (17 at the third, March 1985); an AI staff in the CIA by 1986. [CIA reading room](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/search/site/%22artificial%20intelligence%22)
- **1983 to 1993: DARPA's Strategic Computing,** over $1 billion toward machine intelligence. [Strategic Computing Initiative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strategic_Computing_Initiative)
- **June 9, 1983: the Army's assessment of the Monroe Institute's Gateway Process,** declassified 2003 in the CIA's STARGATE collection.
  [CIA reading room](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/print/271687)
- **1985: Max Headroom,** the "first computer-generated TV presenter," is an actor in makeup. [Max Headroom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Headroom)
- **1985: the Cray-2,** 1.9 gigaflops, the world's fastest.
- **1987 to 1988: the Majestic 12 papers;** the Air Force's investigators call them fabricated, the FBI "completely bogus".
  [MuckRock](https://www.muckrock.com/news/archives/2017/jun/05/fbi-majestic-12)
- **1990 to 1999: the Pentagon studies fake voice and video for psychological operations;** Los Alamos fakes a general's voice. UNVERIFIED in its
  details (which general). [Privacy SOS](https://privacysos.org/blog/technology-to-spoof-human-voices-has-been-around-for-over-fifteen-years/)
- **1991: DART,** a military AI planner said to have paid back DARPA's 30 years of AI money. [Hedberg 2002](https://www.gwern.net/doc/ai/2002-hedberg.pdf)
- **1994: Forrest Gump** puts Tom Hanks beside JFK by compositing. [ILM](https://www.ilm.com/vfx/forrest-gump/)
- **1995: STARGATE ends;** remote viewing not shown to work or used operationally. [CIA reading room](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/node/304501)
- **1997: Video Rewrite,** the first automatic lip-sync fake, in the open. [the paper](https://www.mit.edu/~9.520/spring09/Papers/VideoRewrite.pdf)
- **1997: The Day After Roswell** claims chips, lasers and fiber optics came from Roswell; no documentation found. [The Day After Roswell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Day_After_Roswell)

## 2000 to 2016: secret machine learning at scale

- **2000 to 2002: ASCI White (4.9 teraflops) and the Earth Simulator (35.9 teraflops).** [TOP500 2000](https://top500.org/lists/top500/list/2000/11)
- **2002 to 2003: Total Information Awareness,** defunded in public, reportedly continued at the NSA. [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2006/04/26/229286/the-total-information-awareness-project-lives-on/)
- **2003: the CIA plans a fake Saddam video (never made) and makes a fake bin Laden video with its own employees as actors** (Washington Post 2010,
  via secondary outlets). [Harper's](https://harpers.org/2010/05/cias-brainstorm-saddam-and-gomorrah), [The Daily Beast](https://www.thedailybeast.com/the-cias-saddam-gay-sex-tape/)
- **2003: DARPA's CALO,** the seed of Siri (Apple, 2010 to 2011). [SRI](https://www.sri.com/case-studies/siri/)
- **About 2005 to 2006: the NSA's "Google for Voice"** turns intercepted calls into searchable text. [The Intercept](https://theintercept.com/2015/05/05/nsa-speech-recognition-snowden-searchable-text/)
- **2007 to 2012: AATIP,** about $22 million for UFO research, much of it to Bigelow Aerospace. [NPR](https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2017/12/17/571446881/secret-pentagon-program-spent-millions-to-research-ufos)
- **2008 to 2009: Roadrunner (the first petaflop) and Jaguar (1.75 petaflops, paid for by the NSA);** a classified codebreaking machine follows at Oak
  Ridge (Wired, 2012). [TOP500 2008](https://top500.org/lists/top500/2008/06/), [HPCwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/2012/03/19/nsa_employs_cutting-edge_supercomputing_for_domestic_surveillance/)
- **About 2010: the NRO's Sentient,** a classified AI that tracks targets and re-tasks satellites. [Sentient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sentient_(intelligence_analysis_system))
- **2011 to 2016: IARPA's Babel** (speech recognition for any language); **2014: Janus** (face recognition). [IARPA](https://www.iarpa.gov/who-we-are/history/our-programs)
- **2012: SKYNET,** NSA machine learning over 55 million Pakistani phones. [The Intercept](https://theintercept.com/document/2015/05/08/skynet-courier/)
- **2013: the black budget,** $52.6 billion. [Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2013/08/30/chart-of-the-week-the-black-budget/)
- **2014: GCHQ's "Art of Deception,"** the online covert action manual. [The Intercept](https://theintercept.com/2014/02/24/jtrig-manipulation/)
- **2016: DARPA's Media Forensics,** the government's fake-hunting program. [DARPA](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/media-forensics)
